                                     Narrative Description of School District Proposal


1. What action is the school district proposing to take?
  The school district is proposing the supports, services and placement outlined in the enclosed IEP and Placement forms.


2. Why is the school district proposing to act?
  The school district met with the parents and school-based Team on 11/17/25 for a regularly scheduled Annual Review
Team Meeting. The Team reviewed Marcus's progress and continued areas of need and developed the enclosed IEP for
continued support and services. Marcus continues to qualify under the Specific Learning Disability category, with a
specific profile consistent with Dyscalculia.


3. What rejected options were considered and why was each option rejected?
  Substantially separate placement for Mathematics was discussed and rejected. Marcus is succeeding in general
education for ELA, History, and Science, and the Team believes a daily co-taught Math block plus weekly EF
strategy pull-out is the least restrictive environment that meets his needs.


4. What evaluation procedure, test, record or report was used as a basis for the proposed action?
  - Most recent triennial reevaluation (06/2024): WJ-IV Achievement, WISC-V cognitive (FSIQ 108, with notable
    Working Memory and Processing Speed splits below the Verbal Comprehension and Fluid Reasoning indices).
  - iReady Diagnostic (Math) Fall 2025: overall 5th-grade equivalent; Number & Operations (Fractions) at 4th-
    grade equivalent.
  - iReady Diagnostic (Reading) Fall 2025: at or above 9th-grade equivalent across all sub-domains.
  - Classroom-based work samples (Algebra 1 unit assessments and weekly formative quizzes).
  - Math anxiety screener (MARS-A short form) — elevated, with avoidance-typology profile.
  - Teacher observations from co-teaching Math and weekly counseling sessions.


5. What other factors were relevant to the school district's decision?
  Parent input emphasized Marcus's strong reading and writing combined with persistent math frustration at home,
  particularly around timed assignments and "checking" loops where he asks repeatedly whether his answer is correct.


6. What next steps, if any, are recommended?
  Implement the enclosed IEP through 11/2026. Reconvene mid-year (April 2026) to review progress on
  fraction-operations and multi-step word-problem goals.
