# Quick Accommodations — Jasmine Bailey (bystander-effect read-and-respond)

**Heads-up:** the planned activity asks Jasmine to read a Lexile ~1040 text independently. She reads at a 3rd-grade level overall, Informational Text Comprehension at 2nd grade (IEP: plaafp_academics). Independent comprehension is the documented breakdown point; that 12 minutes is where shutdown will most likely start.

## 1. Top 3 accommodations to actually use right now (verbatim from IEP)

- **Copy of teacher's notes** (IEP: accommodations) — Pre-print her copy with the central-idea sentence and 2 key-detail sentences pre-highlighted before class. She reads the *highlighted* version while peers read the original; she's still answering the same 4 short-response items.
- **Reference sheets, graphic organizers and checklists** (IEP: accommodations) — Clip a 2-column "claim ➜ evidence" organizer to her packet, with the 4 question stems pre-printed in the left column.
- **Extra time + 1:1 check ins** (IEP: accommodations) — Plan one 20-second check-in at minute 6 of the silent read ("Show me one underline so far") and a second before she writes question 1. Allow her to finish during advisory if she runs over the 27-minute block.

## 2. Two micro-modifications for this activity

- **Pre-teach 4 words at her desk in the 90 seconds before "go"**: *bystander, diffusion, intervene, witness* — write each on a sticky note with a one-line kid definition. *(UDL 2.1: Clarify vocabulary, symbols, and language structures, IEP: plaafp_academics)*
- **Chunk the read into 3 mini-segments with a silent desk-tap between each** (~half-page chunks, ~4 min each) — at each tap she circles one detail before reading on. Converts a 12-min independent block into 3 sustainable chunks and counters the shutdown pattern. *(UDL 8.2: Optimize challenge and support, IEP: plaafp_behavioral)*

## 3. Watch-for

If pencil-down + head dipping toward forearm by minute 5 of the silent read, **tap her desk corner** (pre-agreed signal) and point to her shutdown-protocol card — she picks one of her three taught calming strategies (IEP: goals_counseling) and re-enters with a one-task instruction ("Underline one sentence in paragraph 2"). Do **not** ask "what's wrong" — that's the documented failure pattern.
