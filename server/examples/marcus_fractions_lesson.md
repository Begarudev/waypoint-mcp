## 2. Lesson at a glance

- **Objective:** Add and subtract two fractions with unlike denominators by replacing each fraction with an equivalent fraction with a common denominator, then operating on the numerators.

- **Estimated duration:** 50 minutes.

## 5. Scaffolded question ladder
**Central procedural target (5.NF.A.1):** Why and how do we use a common denominator to add or subtract fractions with unlike denominators?

- **DOK 1 — Recall (with sentence stems for Marcus):**
  *Question:* "What is the LCD of 1/4 and 2/3?"
  *Stems:*
  - "The LCD of 1/4 and 2/3 is ______ because both 4 and 3 divide into ______."
  - "I rewrite 1/4 as ___/12 and 2/3 as ___/12."

- **DOK 2 — Apply:**
  "Use the LCD procedure to compute 3/8 + 1/6. Show your common denominator, both rewritten fractions, and your final answer in simplest form."

- **DOK 3 — Analyze:**
  "Pedro added 1/2 + 1/3 and got 2/5 because he added the tops and the bottoms. Using a fraction strip OR a real-world example, explain in 2 sentences *why* Pedro's answer cannot be right and what step he skipped."

| *Simplest form? Yes. ✓ once.* | **Step 5.** SIMPLIFY. CHECK once. Done. | "11 and 12 share no common factor. 11/12 is close to 1 — estimate matches. Done." |
